## 2. ☁️ OpenMetal Launches XL v5 Dedicated Servers With Intel TDX Confidential Computing

OpenMetal announced the immediate availability of its XL v5 bare-metal server tier featuring integrated Intel Trust Domain Extensions (TDX) confidential computing. Unlike VM-level encryption, TDX operates at the hypervisor level on OpenStack Nova, protecting bare-metal instances from unauthorized memory access. The XL v5 targets financial services, healthcare, and sovereign cloud customers who require hardware-enforced confidentiality without sacrificing single-tenant bare-metal performance. Deployments are live in OpenMetal's Amsterdam data center with additional regions planned for Q4 2026.

**Source:** [Intel TDX Confidential VMs Run on OpenMetal Dedicated Bare Metal Hardware — XL v5](https://openmetal.io/resources/blog/infrastructure-as-a-service-iaas/)

## 5. 🌐 ACM SIGCOMM 2026 Presents "Pegasus": A Data Center Network Designed for Bare-Metal AI Cloud

At SIGCOMM 2026 in Denver, researchers presented Pegasus, a novel data center network architecture explicitly designed for bare-metal AI cloud environments. Pegasus offloads per-packet load balancing and credit-based congestion control to smart RNICs (RDMA-capable NICs), optimizing reorder delay and maximizing throughput for multi-node bare-metal GPU clusters. The paper demonstrated 3x improvement in collective communication efficiency compared to traditional Clos topologies, making it highly relevant for organizations deploying scale-out bare-metal AI inference pipelines requiring deterministic low-latency networks.

**Source:** [Pegasus: A Data Center Network for Bare-Metal AI Cloud — ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3789240.3829186)
