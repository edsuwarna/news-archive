## 📊 Summary Themes

### 🖥️ EPYC Venice Takes Center Stage
The week's news is dominated by AMD's 6th Gen EPYC "Venice" platform launch activity. Multiple OEMs — ASUS, Giga Computing, Supermicro, and MSI — are all rolling out EPYC 9006 servers with SP7/SP8 sockets, liquid cooling options, and up to 256 cores. With PCIe Gen 6, 1.6TB/s memory bandwidth, and the new Verano LPDDR node for GPU-attached memory, Venice is positioning as the most consequential server CPU launch of the year.

### ⚡ The AI GPU Arms Race: AMD Helios vs. NVIDIA Vera Rubin
AMD fired back at NVIDIA with the MI455X (432GB HBM4, 40.26 PF) and the 72-GPU Helios rack, directly targeting NVIDIA's Vera Rubin NVL72. Meanwhile, NVIDIA continues revealing Vera CPU benchmarks and expanding its Jetson Thor edge lineup. Wistron's $700M Texas plant becoming the first US site to build GB300 superchips underscores the reshoring of AI hardware manufacturing.

### 🏭 Data Center Infrastructure at Scale
Massive infrastructure projects dominate the build-out side: Brookfield's gigawatt campus at a Kentucky nuclear site, Talen's 23-building campus in Pennsylvania, and Axe Compute's $1.5B compute contract signal that the AI-driven demand for data center capacity shows no signs of slowing. On the storage front, KIOXIA's liquid-cooled SSD, NetApp's DataPelago acquisition, and VAST Data's AMD collaboration all point toward storage architectures evolving to keep pace with GPU compute density.
