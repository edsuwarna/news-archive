### 2. K8gb Enters CNCF Incubator as Global Load Balancer for Kubernetes

The CNCF Technical Oversight Committee voted to accept Kubernetes Global Balancer (K8gb) as a CNCF incubating project. K8gb provides cloud native DNS-based global server load balancing (GSLB) for Kubernetes, enabling active-active multi-cluster traffic routing across geographic regions. It supports AWS Route53, Google Cloud DNS, Azure DNS, and Cloudflare as backend providers, and integrates seamlessly with ingress controllers including NGINX and Traefik. The project was previously accepted to CNCF Sandbox in March 2021 and moved through the Incubator acceptance process after demonstrating broad industry support and a healthy contributor base from multiple organizations.

**Source:** [CNCF Announcement](https://www.cncf.io/announcements/2026/08/05/k8gb-becomes-a-cncf-incubating-project/)

## 🐧 Linux Foundation

### 7. Xen Project Launches Shared Functional Safety Initiative for Safety-Critical Systems

The Xen Project, an open source hypervisor platform hosted by the Linux Foundation, announced the creation of the Xen Safety Committee, a collaborative body designed to develop reusable safety engineering artifacts for functional-safety certification across automotive, industrial automation, and avionics domains. Working with founding partners AMD, EPAM, and Renesas, the committee maintains a shared foundation of safety documentation, failure mode analyses, and certification evidence that participating companies can adapt rather than rebuilding from scratch. This initiative addresses one of the biggest barriers to deploying open source virtualization in safety-certified environments — the prohibitive cost and effort required to produce independent safety assessments. Premier Plus members gain early access to the committee's maintained artifact library, which targets IEC 61508, ISO 26262, and DO-178C standards.

**Source:** [Linux Foundation Press Release](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/xen-project-launches-shared-functional-initiative-for-safety-critical-systems)
