# Top AI News — August 17, 2026

## 1. 🇪🇺 EU AI Act Transparency Rules Take Effect
On August 2, 2026, the most consequential obligations of the EU AI Act came into force. Article 50 requires providers and deployers of certain AI systems to disclose when users are interacting with machines, label synthetic media and deepfakes, and comply with Annex III high-risk system requirements. The European Commission published finalised guidelines in July, and national authorities across the EU must now establish at least one AI regulatory sandbox by this date. This marks the first time comprehensive AI transparency rules have been enforced globally.

**Source:** [Safer and more transparent AI](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/safer-and-more-transparent-ai-2026-08-02_en)

## 2. 🏢 Google DeepMind Leadership Shakeup
On August 5, 2026, Google announced a major leadership reset at Google DeepMind. **Source:** [Shakes Up A.I. Leadership — The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/05/technology/google-ai-leadership.html)

## 3. 🤖 Meta Launches Muse Glimmer — Open-Weight Agentic Model
On August 10, 2026, Meta unveiled Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter causal language model purpose-built for local, always-on agent workflows. The release gives developers access to a perception encoder with dedicated multimodal capabilities that rival frontier API models.

**Source:** [Introducing Muse Glimmer — Meta Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model)

## 5. 💧 Anthropic Adds Invisible Watermarks to Claude Output
Effective August 2, 2026, future Claude models embed invisible watermarks directly into generated text to help detect AI-generated content. Signed C2PA provenance metadata is attached to files. The move responds to both EU AI Act compliance and growing industry pressure around AI transparency. Other major model developers have signed the same voluntary code commitment. Future Claude models will carry the watermark, though currently-deployed versions may not yet be updated.

**Source:** [How Claude's text watermarking works — Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark)
