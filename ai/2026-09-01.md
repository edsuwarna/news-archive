## 🔒 Security & Regulation

### 13. EU AI Act Enforcement Begins: Transparency Rules Take Effect
**Aug 2** · The **European Commission's AI Office** officially began enforcing provisions of the EU AI Act starting **August 2, 2026**. New transparency rules require businesses to disclose when users are interacting with AI chatbots or machines, mark synthetic content appropriately, and comply with conformity assessments for high-risk AI systems. CE marking now mandatory for covered products. Providers of General-Purpose AI models must publish detailed summaries of training data. The Digital Omnibus amendment further clarifies compliance timelines. First enforcement actions expected in Q4 2026.

🔗 [EU Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/safer-and-more-transparent-ai-2026-08-02_en) · [Cooley Alert](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026) · [Artificial Intelligence Act EU](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/)

### 14. INTERPOL: AI Linked to Over Half of Cybercrime in Africa
**Aug 3** · INTERPOL's African Cyberthreat Assessment Report 2026 found that **AI was used in 55% of reported cybercrimes** across surveyed African nations. Recommended interventions include cross-border AI forensics capabilities and international model-access monitoring.
