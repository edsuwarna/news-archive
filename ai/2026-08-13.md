## 1. 🤖 Meta Launches Muse Glimmer — Open-Weight Agentic Model for Local PCs [Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/metas-new-glimmer-ai-model-offers-a-hint-at-zuckerbergs-personal-intelligence-vision/)

Meta Superintelligence Labs unveiled Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter causal language model designed to run directly on consumer laptops and desktops without cloud dependencies. Distilled from the closed-source Muse Spark model released in July, Glimmer features a dedicated perception encoder, a 120K+ token context window, and is licensed under Apache 2.0. It represents Meta's return to the open-weight ecosystem alongside Zuckerberg's vision of personal AI intelligence that runs locally on users' devices. Compatible with AMD Ryzen AI Max and NVIDIA hardware, Muse Glimmer enables local agentic workflows at a fraction of the cost of cloud inference.
