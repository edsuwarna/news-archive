### 17. EU AI Act Enforcement Phase Begins — Transparency Rules Take Effect
**Aug 2** · A new phase of the **EU Artificial Intelligence Act** came into force. New transparency obligations require AI providers to disclose AI-generated content and mark synthetic media. The AI Office and national authorities began exercising enforcement powers. High-risk regime rules remain deferred. Member states must establish national AI regulatory sandboxes. Compliance requirements include labeling and documentation for AI-generated content.
