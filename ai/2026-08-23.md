## ⚖️ Regulation & Policy

### 12. EU AI Act Enforcement Phase Begins — Transparency Rules Take Effect
**Aug 2** · A new phase of the **EU Artificial Intelligence Act** came into force on August 2, 2026. New transparency obligations require AI providers to disclose AI-generated content and mark synthetic media. The AI Office, together with national authorities, began exercising enforcement powers. High-risk regime rules remain deferred. Member states must establish national AI regulatory sandboxes by this deadline. Companies face compliance requirements for labeling and documentation.
