## 📊 Summary Themes

**Theme 1: AI Agents Move from Hype to Real-World Infrastructure Challenges**
AI agents dominate this edition, from Microsoft's Build announcements of Docker Compose for Agents on Azure Container Apps, to AWS's agentic AI solutions for Amazon Connect, and InfoQ's deep-dive on why agent context is fundamentally an infrastructure problem. However, despite aggressive vendor positioning ("era of AI agents"), enterprise customers remain hesitant, revealing a widening gap between vendor enthusiasm and practical adoption realities. Organizations are increasingly focused on the operational plumbing — latency, staleness, sync costs, and evaluation — rather than just model selection.

**Theme 2: Cloud Providers Race to Expand Managed Kubernetes & Observability Capabilities**
Microsoft extended AKS with bare metal and fleet management, AWS made CloudWatch OpenTelemetry/PromQL support GA with 23 new logs insights commands, and multiple articles highlight the ongoing battle among AWS EKS (31% market share), Google GKE (~25%), and Azure AKS for managed Kubernetes dominance. The emphasis is clearly on making Kubernetes easier to operate at scale while reducing cognitive load through better observability tooling and standardized instrumentation protocols.

**Theme 3: Security Shifts Left with Post-Quantum Ready + Green DevOps Momentum**
Security and sustainability are converging as core DevOps concerns. Cloudflare's post-quantum authentication for origins prepares infrastructure for future cryptographic threats, GitLab's carbon-aware CI/CD gives teams visibility into environmental impact, and infoQ reports on Cloudflare scaling Infrastructure as Code with shift-left security practices across hundreds of production accounts. The trend points toward automated, measurable governance baked directly into the delivery pipeline rather than bolted on afterward.
