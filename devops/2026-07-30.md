## 📊 Summary Themes

### 🛡️ Security & Supply Chain Dominance
The overwhelming theme this week is security — from post-quantum authentication and supply chain attack disruptions to AI agent intrusions and FakeGit campaigns. The industry is racing to secure the AI-assisted software supply chain while simultaneously leveraging AI for defense (Codex Security CLI, Precursor). The HuggingFace intrusion timeline and GitHub's anti-supply-chain measures signal that infosec is the #1 priority for platform teams.

### 🤖 AI Agent Infrastructure Matures
A major cluster of stories revolves around the infrastructure required to run AI agents at scale: Netflix's LLM serving platform, Microsoft's multi-layer routing on AKS, Grafana's AI assistant expansion, and Cloudflare's Precursor for detecting agentic behavior. The "how to serve and manage AI" question is becoming as critical as "how to build AI."

### 🌐 Internet Resilience & Infrastructure
Cloudflare's Q2 disruption report, BGP attribute analysis, Internal DNS GA, and Cache Response Rules all point to a continued focus on making the Internet more resilient and observable. DNSSEC failures, government shutdowns, and transit provider manipulation remain top-of-mind for SRE teams managing global infrastructure.

### ⚡ DevOps Investment Acceleration
Industry data shows DevOps tooling investments accelerating in the AI era, with platform engineering emerging as the primary organizational model. CodePen's evolution into a deployment platform and the debate around shipping AI-generated code without human review reflect a sector in rapid transformation.
