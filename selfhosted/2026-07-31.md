## 🔥 Recap Minggu Ini

- **AI & agents tetap mendominasi** — LLM routers (Millwright), agent sandboxes (AgentNest), multi-tenant agents (Oto-Dock), dan AI intranet (Autonomous Grid) jadi tren utama.
- **Privacy-first alternatif SaaS naik daun** — CCTV (CheapSecurity), document signing (LetsSeal), fitness tracker (openGym, Dreeve), dan analytics (Edgemetry) semua bisa self-hosted sekarang.
- **Homelab utilities makin dewasa** — dari process supervisor (Servicrab), WireGuard management (Islandr), sampai direktori app (Selfhost.Directory), ekosistem homelab makin lengkap.
- **Big tech ikut main** — Tencent merilis Octop, tanda kalau self-hosting jadi mainstream.
